1. Android Permissions Disclosure (Google Play Policy)

The Netwide Android application (com.netwide.mobile) requests the following hardware permissions strictly for local RF and network diagnostics:

  • android.permission.ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION, ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION, and NEARBY_WIFI_DEVICES: Required by the Android OS API (WifiManager.getScanResults()) to inspect nearby 802.11 Wi-Fi beacon frames, BSSID hardware addresses, signal strength (RSSI dBm), channel width, TSF timestamps, and Information Elements (IEs) for the 8-Stage Hidden Wi-Fi De-Cloaker and RF Geiger Counter. Netwide never tracks or transmits your GPS coordinates to any remote server.
  • android.permission.ACCESS_WIFI_STATE & CHANGE_WIFI_STATE: Used to trigger active 802.11 Wi-Fi scans and read current link layer telemetry.
  • android.permission.INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Used to communicate directly withdevices on your local subnet (mDNS, SSDP, SNMP, Cast, HomeKit, Kasa, PlayStation DDP) and optionally connect to your self-hosted Netwide Guardian Server on your local LAN.
  • Magnetometer / Sensor Access: Used locally in real time to display magnetic flux density (μT) for the ferromagnetic coil & hidden electronics detector.

2. Data Storage & Retention

All discovered device records, 24-hour presence heatmaps, security alerts, and subscription license keys are stored locally on your device (`localStorage` / Android WebView storage) or in your self-hosted SQLite database (~/.config/netwide/netwide_history.db). You can purge all historical records at any time from the Workbench or CLI.
